## Chapter Briefs

### Chapter 1: The Trap (100% new)

**Part:** I: The Trap
**Thesis:** You're a director at work and a disaster at home — and the AI economy is making it worse, not better. The cognitive demands of the new economy are unprecedented, and they're draining the capacity you need for everything else.

**Thread assignments:**

- Stable ground argument [HIGH]: Why build at home? Because home is the only ground that doesn't shift under you.
- Complicated vs. complex [HIGH]: Your job is complex (unpredictable, adaptive). Your home CAN be complicated (manageable, systematic). The old playbook treats both as complicated.
- Author's origin story: The dishrag moment — three weeks into the AI factory, coming home depleted.
- Death spiral introduced: Work drains capacity -> home runs on fumes -> less capacity for work -> spiral tightens.
- Promotion metaphor introduced: "You wouldn't run a department the way you run your life."
- Third path introduced [FIRST TOUCH]: Not the grind path, not the escape path. The architecture path.

**Key scenes to write:**

- The author's first three weeks in the AI factory
- The dishrag moment (coming home depleted, the realization)
- The professional/personal infrastructure gap (can reference the labnotes comparison)
