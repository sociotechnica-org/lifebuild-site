## Book-Level Failure Modes

### 1. Template fatigue in Part III

**Defense:** Vary emotional register across Ch 4-7. The predictability of structure frees the reader to focus on content.

### 2. Narrative momentum gap in Part III

**Defense:** Author's Part III stories should form a connected arc (board → team → crash → rhythm). **New author input item (#19):** Did these happen in sequence?

### 3. The book feels like more to do

**Defense:** Add meta-moment in Ch 7: "Look at the light path across all four chapters. A brain dump. Two delegations. One blue per day. A twenty-minute weekly review. It runs on less time than you spend lying awake at night."

### 4. AI content dates the book

**Defense:** Frame all AI references as current-state: "Today's AI tools can handle X" not "AI can handle X." Add to ghostwriter master instructions.

### 5. Closing is too quiet

**Defense:** Ch 9 Beat 6 must be exquisitely precise. Brevity is a feature only if earned through precision.
