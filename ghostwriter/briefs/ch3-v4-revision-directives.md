## Directive 1: Relocate the Supplement Example from Beat 3 to Beat 4

**Problem:** The supplement anecdote sits in Beat 3 (Target Selection) but teaches resolution, not selection. Its content -- "sat down with an AI chat, figured out the actual cadence, set up auto-subscriptions" -- is a description of the Automate method. It belongs in Beat 4 under Automate, not in Beat 3 between the prompts and "Pick one."

**The fix:**

**In Beat 3:** Delete the full supplement paragraph entirely (from "I had a supplement ordering problem" through "not having to think about it").

**In Beat 4, Automate section:** Insert the supplement story as a one-sentence illustration, integrated into the existing examples. The revised Automate section should read:

> **Automate.** Some thorns are recurring irritations that could run without you. Bills you keep forgetting to pay -- set up autopay. Supplements you keep reordering by hand across four websites -- set up subscriptions and stop thinking about it. Decisions you keep remaking -- create a default. You invest time once, and the problem stops existing.

## Directive 2: Trim Beat 3 Prompt Follow-ups and Cut Closing Examples

**Problem:** Each of the four prompts runs 2-3 sentences where 1 would do. The three closing examples after "Be specific" duplicate what the prompts already did.

**The fix, prompt by prompt:**

**Prompt 1:**

> _What have you been avoiding for more than a month?_ The stuff you've been actively not-doing -- the task that makes you wince when you remember it exists.

**Prompt 2:**

> _What commitment do you resent every time it appears?_ The obligation you said yes to and immediately regretted.

**Prompt 3:**

> _What's generating dread disproportionate to the task?_ Something about it has grown larger than it deserves to be.

**Prompt 4:** Keep as-is. Already tight.

**Closing:**

> Pick one. Write it down. Be specific -- not "get healthier" or "be more organized," but the concrete thing with a name and a deadline.
>
> One thorn. Written down. That's your target.

## Directive 3: Compress Beat 4 Accountability Email and Eliminate Section

**Part A:** Replace the entire accountability paragraph with:

> Want some help? Send me an email -- subject line "Accountability Support Request," your commitment in the body -- and I'll check in on you in 72 hours: danvers@sociotechnica.org.

**Part B:** Revised Eliminate section:

> **Eliminate.** Some thorns shouldn't exist. The commitment that never should have been made. The obligation that serves no one. The thing you've been doing out of habit or guilt or a vague sense that you "should."
>
> Elimination is the most underused option. We're trained to solve problems, not dissolve them. The discomfort of saying "I can't continue with this" is real, but it's a one-time cost in exchange for eliminating a recurring drain.
